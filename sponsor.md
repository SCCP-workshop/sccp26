# Call for Sponsorship

The **1st Symposium on Consistency Checking Principles (SCCP 2026)** will be held
in Boston on September 04, 2026, in conjunction with
[VLDB 2026](https://vldb.org/2026/).

SCCP is a new venue dedicated to the formal verification, runtime checking, and
principled analysis of consistency models in databases, distributed systems, and
concurrent programs. As the inaugural edition of the workshop, SCCP 2026 brings
together researchers and practitioners from distributed systems, programming
languages, formal methods, and databases to systematize existing work, unify
terminology, and identify directions for future research.

We invite companies, research laboratories, and institutions to support SCCP
2026. Sponsorship makes the workshop accessible to students and early-career
researchers, and connects sponsors directly with a focused community working on
problems that matter in production systems: transaction isolation, replication,
memory models, and the tools that check them.

## Why Sponsor SCCP

* **A focused, technical audience.** SCCP gathers people who work specifically
  on consistency and its verification: an area where relevant expertise is
  scarce and hard to recruit for.
* **Co-location with VLDB.** SCCP takes place alongside one of the premier
  international database conferences, giving sponsors visibility within the
  broader database community.
* **Ground-floor visibility.** As the first edition of SCCP, sponsors are
  recognized as founding supporters of the venue.
* **Direct support for students.** Sponsorship funds student travel and
  participation, bringing new researchers into the field.

## Sponsorship Benefits

Sponsorship is organized into tiers. The benefits below apply automatically at
levels determined by tier; additional benefits are available upon request and
subject to coordination with the organizers. Please
[contact us](#becoming-a-sponsor) to discuss tiers and the package that best
fits your organization's goals.

### Included Benefits

1. **Homepage logo placement.**
   Prominent, tier-based logo placement on the workshop homepage, which serves
   as the primary entry point for prospective authors, program committee
   members, speakers, and attendees.

2. **Logo placement on the CFP and promotional materials.**
   Logo inclusion on the Call for Papers webpage, PDF, and poster. Because these
   materials circulate through mailing lists, research groups, institutional
   channels, and social media, they provide visibility beyond the workshop's
   registered attendees.

3. **Recognition on opening and closing slides.**
   Sponsor logos will appear during the workshop's opening and closing sessions,
   when the full audience is assembled.

4. **Logo placement on the printed program.**
   Sponsor logos may appear on the workshop program distributed to attendees and
   used as a reference throughout the event.

5. **Named student travel awards.**
   Sponsor-funded travel support may be presented as the "⟨Company⟩ Student
   Travel Award," with recipients recognized on the workshop website. This
   opportunity directly supports student participation and engagement.

6. **Recruiting profile and employment links.**
   The sponsor profile may include a recruiting description and links to
   relevant open positions, providing direct access to candidates with expertise
   in consistency, verification, databases, and systems.

7. **Recruiting materials at the sponsor table.**
   Sponsors may provide printed descriptions of open roles, internships,
   research opportunities, and technical teams for display at the designated
   sponsor table.

8. **Opt-in attendee contact information.**
   Where permitted, sponsors may receive contact information from attendees who
   explicitly consent to sponsor communications. All collection and sharing will
   follow applicable privacy requirements and conference policies.

9. **Introductions to student presenters.**
   Organizers may facilitate introductions between sponsor representatives and
   consenting student presenters whose work aligns with the sponsor's technical
   or recruiting interests.

### Optional Benefits (Upon Request)

1. **Dedicated sponsor profile.**
   A permanent sponsor profile featuring the organization's logo, a 50--100-word
   description, and a link to its website. This profile allows the sponsor to
   communicate its mission, research interests, and relevant activities directly
   to the workshop community.

2. **Named Best Paper Award.**
   A sponsor may support a named award, such as the "⟨Company⟩ Best Paper Award
   at SCCP 2026." The award will be announced at the workshop and recorded on
   the workshop website.

3. **Sponsor information table.**
   Sponsors may provide brochures, technical materials, recruiting information,
   and approved promotional items for display at a designated table.

4. **Sponsored coffee break or lunch.**
   A sponsor may receive dedicated recognition for supporting a coffee break or
   meal, subject to coordination with VLDB and the conference catering provider.

5. **Sponsor lightning talk.**
   A five-minute program slot for the sponsor to introduce its organization,
   relevant technical work, and engagement with the workshop's research area.

6. **Panel participation.**
   A sponsor representative may participate in a panel with academic and
   industry researchers on topics such as consistency checking in production
   systems.

7. **Opportunity to propose an open-problems topic.**
   Sponsors may suggest technical challenges for consideration in the workshop's
   open-problems discussion. Final topic selection and framing will remain under
   organizer control.
