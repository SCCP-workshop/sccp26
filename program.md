# Program

SCCP 2026 will be held on **September 4, 2026** in Boston, co-located with
[VLDB 2026](https://vldb.org/2026/).

Every paper needs at least one author with a regular workshop registration
(workshop, or conference &amp; workshop). See the
[VLDB 2026 registration page](https://vldb.org/2026/registration.html).
